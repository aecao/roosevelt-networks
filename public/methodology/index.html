Roosevelt Networks

Developed by Anna Cao. Dataset built and populated by Anna Cao, Victoria Li, and Audrey Lau.

ARCH 5115 / Designing the Urban Commons / Garine Boghossian and Kobi Ruthenberg

Cornell University, Fall 2026

Roosevelt Networks is an interactive model of the actors that shape Roosevelt Island (agencies, organizations, institutions, buildings and people) and the relationships between them. This documentation explains how to use the app, how the model is constructed, and how to load your own dataset.

Actors

Each node in the model is an actor: any party with a stake in or influence on the island. Actors are recorded in the project dataset with the following attributes:

  • Sector: the kind of actor, used for the Sector filter and colour mode.
  • Category: the topic(s) the actor deals with, such as governance, housing, transit or ecology.
  • Scale: micro, meso or macro, describing the reach of the actor's influence.
  • Active years: an optional start and end year, used by the Time range filter and Timeline mode. Missing years are treated as open-ended.
  • Public sentiment: a rating of how the actor is perceived, shown in the Public sentiment colour mode.
  • Public interest: the number of news results for the actor, used as a proxy for public visibility in the halo.

Relationships

Connections between actors are typed. Collaboration is treated as mutual (no direction); every other type is directional and drawn with an arrow.

Centrality

Halo size and the Centrality filter can use four standard network measures, calculated on the network currently shown:

  • Degree: how many direct connections an actor has.
  • Closeness: how few steps, on average, it takes to reach every other actor.
  • Betweenness: how often an actor lies on the shortest path between two others, i.e. how much it acts as a bridge.
  • Eigenvector: how well connected an actor's connections are, a measure of prestige.

Map

Building footprints come from New York City open data and are identified by BIN (Building Identification Number), with lot-level attributes from PLUTO. Each footprint can be linked to an actor in the dataset's buildings sheet. Sites without a building footprint, such as parks and ferry landings, are placed as points traced over the island boundary.
