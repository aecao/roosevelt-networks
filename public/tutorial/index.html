Roosevelt Networks

Developed by Anna Cao. Dataset built and populated by Anna Cao, Victoria Li, and Audrey Lau.

ARCH 5115 / Designing the Urban Commons / Garine Boghossian and Kobi Ruthenberg

Cornell University, Fall 2026

Roosevelt Networks is an interactive model of the actors that shape Roosevelt Island (agencies, organizations, institutions, buildings and people) and the relationships between them. This documentation explains how to use the app, how the model is constructed, and how to load your own dataset.

Modes

Use the tabs at the top of the window to switch between three views of the same dataset:

  • Relationships lays the network out with a force-directed layout, so closely connected actors cluster together.
  • Map places actors on the buildings and sites they are linked to on Roosevelt Island. Hover over an unlinked building footprint to see its name or BIN.
  • Timeline (beta) plots actors across their active years and links predecessors to their successors.

Selecting, hovering and pinning

  • Hover over an actor or connection to highlight it and its direct neighbours; everything else fades back.
  • Click an actor or connection to open its details in the right-hand Details panel. Click empty space to clear the selection.
  • Pin a selection to keep it in the Details panel while you explore other actors. Pinned items can be expanded, collapsed and reordered by dragging.
  • Use the search box to find an actor by full name or abbreviation; each result can be opened or pinned directly.
  • When the Details panel is collapsed, a dot on its button signals a new selection or pin you haven't looked at yet.

Filters and appearance

The left-hand sidebar controls what is shown and how it is drawn:

  • Appearance: toggle labels, set text size and an optional label threshold, set node size, choose a colour mode (sector, category, public sentiment or plain), and turn the halo on or off and choose what its size represents.
  • Centrality: hide actors below a minimum centrality score, using degree, closeness, betweenness or eigenvector centrality.
  • Sector, Category and Scale: show or hide actors by group.
  • Relationships: show or hide each kind of connection, and adjust connection curvature and layout gravity.
  • Time range: show only the actors active in a given year.
  • Freeze actor position stops the layout from moving; Zoom to selected moves the view to each new selection.

Sliders have − and + buttons for stepping through values, and their value can be clicked to type an exact number.

Legend

The Legend button in the header opens a movable, resizable legend describing everything in the current model: relationship types and their line styles, line-weight scales, node colours, scales and halos. Use A− / A+ to change its text size and the fit button to resize it to its contents. Closing the legend collapses it back into the Legend button.

Export

Export saves the current view. Basic export saves an image immediately; Advanced lets you choose raster or vector output, resolution, background, whether to export the viewport or the whole model, how to include the legend, and whether to export only selected/pinned items or dim everything else.

Relinking data

The app starts from a bundled snapshot of the dataset. Relink data fetches the latest version of the published spreadsheet and rebuilds the model. Edits to the spreadsheet can take up to five minutes to be published.
